Getting started

Eggtimer helps you boil eggs to the right firmness, and take eggs out of an order at the right time. You choose what you want to cook, press start, and the app beeps when the eggs are ready.

The times in the app are preliminary. They are adjusted to match how your eggs turn out, see "Adjusting the time" below.

Choose a mode

  • Single egg: one egg with the chosen firmness. The countdown starts when you press start, or when the water boils.
  • Order: several eggs in the same pot, for example 2 soft-boiled, 1 semi-set and 3 hard-boiled. Each group is announced when it is ready.

You can only change mode before you have started.

Choose firmness

  • Soft-boiled: runny yolk, set white.
  • Semi-set: a middle ground. Part of the yolk is set, but nothing is fully runny.
  • Hard-boiled: fully set yolk.

The time for each firmness is shown next to its name.

Choose water

  • Boiling water: the water is already boiling when you add the eggs. The countdown starts immediately.
  • Cold water, water-boils button: you add the eggs to cold water (5–10 °C), press Start, and press "Water boils" when the water boils. The countdown starts then.
  • Cold water, estimate: the app starts the countdown automatically after the time to boil that you have calibrated. Only available after calibration.

Calibrate the pot

Calibration gives a good starting point for the estimate. Do it with the pot and amount of water you usually use:

  1. Choose "Cold water" and press Calibrate pot.
  2. Put the pot with cold water on the stove, and press Start.
  3. Press Water boils when the water boils.

The time to boil is saved. You can calibrate again at any time.

Start, cancel and beeping

  • Start starts the countdown, or waiting for boil with cold water.
  • Cancel stops the countdown. Nothing is saved.
  • Stop beeping stops the beeping and vibration. The egg is still ready.

When the time is up, the app beeps and vibrates on devices that support it. The beeping continues until you stop it. Sound only starts after you have pressed a button, as browsers require.

Order

In order mode all eggs start at the same time. When a group is ready, the app shows its row as "Take out now" and beeps. Press Taken out when you have taken out the group.

  • You cannot add eggs after the order has started. Start a new order for more eggs.
  • Groups that are ready at the same time beep with one beep. Their rows are shown separately.
  • When all groups are taken out, "All eggs are out!" is shown.
  • Feedback and adjustment only apply to single eggs.

Adjusting the time

Eggs vary. Size, temperature and stove all affect the time. After a single egg is ready, choose:

  • Too short: the time increases by 30 seconds next time.
  • Just right: no change.
  • Too long: the time decreases by 30 seconds next time.

The adjustment only applies to the firmness and water method you used, and it cannot exceed 3 minutes in either direction. Under "Adjustment" you can see what is saved. Press Reset adjustment to remove all adjustments.

Language

Choose a language at the top right. Norwegian and English are available. The choice is saved in your browser.

Storage and privacy

Eggtimer has no account, and you do not need to sign in. Calibration, adjustments and language are only saved in the browser on this device. They are not synced between devices. If you clear your browser data, the calibration and adjustments are lost.

Tips

  • Keep the sound on, so you hear the beeping.
  • Use the same pot and amount of water when you calibrate.
  • Try one egg first, and give feedback as you go. The times then fit your kitchen.
